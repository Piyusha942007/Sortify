How Sortify Thinks

A look under the hood. Sortify pipelines emails through an advanced multi-agent categorization loop.

01
Email Arrives
Messages stream in securely from webhooks or sandbox loops.
02
AI Analysis
Gemini Flash inspects details, urgency, and security indicators.
03
Smart Rules
Filters check mapping tables, domain rules, and templates.
04
Categorization
Emails categorized into Important, Clients, Promo, or Updates.
05
Draft Reply
Gemini generates draft templates matching prompts.
06
Gmail Action
Labels get written and drafts appended directly to your inbox.

Sandbox Mode vs Connected Gmail

Compare how the sandbox lets you safely review features before authorizing real mailbox access.

Sandbox Mode Demo Mode
No authentication or credentials required to explore.
Includes simulator widget to trigger emails and phishing alerts dynamically.
Fully persistent SQLite sandbox for editing rules and templates.
Cannot process real incoming mail or read active threads.
Drafts are simulated inside dashboard rather than written to Gmail.
Connected Gmail Real Mode
Syncs live with your real Google mail inbox.
Automated Gmail label generation (categorizes mail inside Gmail).
Appends AI drafted replies directly in your Gmail "Drafts" folder.
Executes real 1-click mailto or HTTP unsubscriptions.
Actively quarantines live phishing attempts inside logs.
